## Making Hits During Ranked Wars

The primary objective of a Ranked War is to earn **Respect (R)** for your faction. Every successful attack contributes toward your faction's total score, and the faction with the highest score at the end of the war wins.

While every successful attack increases the chain count, **not every hit contributes equally**. Understanding when and who to attack is one of the biggest differences between an average war participant and an efficient one.

> Winning a Ranked War isn't about making the most attacks—it's about making the **right attacks**.

## Understanding Attack Types

There are three types of attacks you'll hear mentioned during a Ranked War.

::accordion

:::accordion-item{label="War Hit" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}

A **War Hit** is any successful attack made against a member of the faction you are currently at war with.

These hits:

- Increase your faction's chain.
- Generate Respect for the war.
- Count toward Ranked War scoring.
- Benefit from the automatic Ranked War multiplier.

Unless leadership says otherwise, your primary objective during war is making quality war hits.
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Chain Hit" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
A **Chain Hit** is **any successful attack** that increases the faction chain.

Unlike War Hits, a chain hit **does not have to be against the enemy faction**.

These are commonly made against:

- Inactive players
- Easy targets
- Low-stat opponents
- Existing chain lists

Chain hits are mainly used to:

- Keep the chain alive
- Reach upcoming bonus hits
- Prevent chain cooldowns

::note
During Ranked Wars, leadership may ask members to perform chain hits if maintaining the chain becomes more important than searching for difficult war targets.
::
:::

:::accordion-item{label="Bonus Hit" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}
Bonus Hits are the single most valuable milestones during a chain.

When your faction reaches specific chain numbers, the player landing that exact hit receives a fixed Respect bonus.

Current bonus milestones are:
10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000, 50,000, 100,000

Unlike normal attacks, these bonuses award a **large amount of fixed Respect**, regardless of the target.

Because of this, bonus hits should almost always be made against the **enemy war faction**, not random chain targets.

::warning
Bonus Hits are the **highest priority** during a Ranked War.
Accidentally wasting a bonus hit on a random chain target costs the faction valuable Respect.
::
:::
::

### Holding for Bonus Hits

As your faction approaches an important bonus milestone, leadership may announce:

> **"HOLD"** or **"CALL HITS"**

Holding means:

- Stop attacking immediately.
- Wait for further instructions.
- Do not attempt "one quick hit."
- Stay ready.

Leadership will coordinate the remaining attacks so that the bonus lands on an enemy war target instead of being accidentally consumed by a chain hit.

For example:

| Current Chain | Action                           |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 247           | Leadership calls **Hold**        |
| 248           | Assigned player attacks          |
| 249           | Assigned player attacks          |
| 250           | Bonus hit lands on enemy faction |

Without coordination, someone could accidentally hit a random inactive player and waste the bonus.

::warning
If leadership calls **Hold**, stop immediately—even if you've already found a target.
::

## Choosing the Right Target

One of the biggest mistakes new players make is attacking the **first enemy they see**. Instead, try to attack targets that provide the greatest Respect gain.

Respect depends on several factors, including:

- Fair Fight multiplier
- War multiplier
- Retaliation bonuses
- Warlord bonuses
- Group attack modifiers

The better your target selection, the more valuable every point of Energy becomes.

### Fair Fight (FF)

Every attack in Torn receives a **Fair Fight (FF)** multiplier. The game rewards attacks against opponents who are closer to your own strength. The Fair Fight multiplier ranges approximately from:

| Fair Fight | Result                  |
| ---------- | ----------------------- |
| ~1x        | Very easy opponent      |
| ~2x        | Moderate opponent       |
| ~3x        | Evenly matched opponent |

Higher Fair Fight (2.5x - 3x) means:

- More Respect
- Better Energy efficiency
- Better Ranked War contribution

In general, attacking players with approximately **75% or more of your own total Battle Stats** produces the highest Fair Fight multiplier.

::caution
A harder opponent isn't always the better choice if you cannot defeat them reliably. A successful attack with a slightly lower FF is worth far more than a failed attack.
::

### Battle Stat Projections (BSP)

Before most Ranked Wars, leadership shares **Projected Battle Stats (BSP)** for the opposing faction. These estimates help members decide:

- Which opponents are realistic targets.
- Which players should be avoided.
- Which targets are likely to provide the best Fair Fight multiplier.

Remember that projections are estimates. Actual Battle Stats can differ depending on: Temporary stat gains, Recent training, Merits, Weapon bonuses, Company perks

The best way to verify a target is simply by attacking them. Once you've successfully hit someone, you'll know:

- Their approximate strength.
- The Fair Fight multiplier.
- The Respect gained.

This information becomes useful for future attacks throughout the war.

## Maximizing Respect

Every War Hit already receives Torn's built-in Ranked War multiplier. However, several additional bonuses can further increase Respect earned from each attack. Stacking multiple bonuses together can dramatically increase the value of a single attack.

| Bonus           | Effect                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ranked War      | Automatic                |
| Warlord Weapon  | Additional Respect bonus |
| Fair Fight      | Up to approximately 3×   |
| Retaliation     | 1.5× multiplier          |
| Overseas Attack | 1.25× multiplier         |
| Group Attack    | Up to 1.5× multiplier    |

### Warlord Weapons

Whenever possible, borrow a **Warlord** weapon from the faction armory before attacking. These weapons provide additional Respect during Ranked Wars and are one of the easiest ways to improve your contribution.

If none are available:

- Wait for leadership if possible.
- If necessary, retrieve one from an inactive faction member (typically offline for 15 minutes or longer).
- Return it promptly once you're finished.

::tip
Always message the owner if you temporarily borrow their weapon. Courtesy goes a long way.
::

### Retaliation Hits

When an enemy attacks one of your faction members, Torn creates a short **retaliation window**. During approximately the next **5 minutes**, another faction member can hospitalize that attacker to claim the retaliation bonus.

Successful retaliation attacks receive:

- **1.5× Respect multiplier**

Because of this, retaliation opportunities are often called out in Discord. If you're online and have Energy available, keep an eye on faction chat during active pushes.

### Overseas Attacks

Attacking enemies while both players are overseas grants an additional multiplier. **1.25× Respect**

However, travelling carries additional risk. Before travelling, always consider:

- Is the target actually overseas?
- Can they defeat you?
- Are other enemy members already in that country?
- Will travelling delay your participation?

::caution
Never travel during Ranked Wars unless it supports the current strategy or leadership has approved it.
::

### Group Attacks

Group attacks can significantly increase Respect through additional attack multipliers. However, they also require coordination and consume multiple players' Energy. For this reason, InQuest generally prefers efficient solo attacks unless leadership specifically requests a coordinated group attack.
