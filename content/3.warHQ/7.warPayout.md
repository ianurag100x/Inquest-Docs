At Inquest, the war payout is transparent and rewarding. Once the war is finished and the faction is rewarded with cache's. The leadership will immediately put the cache for sale either on item market or sell them directly to a trusted trader.

::info
Sometimes to get better deals, it may take hours to sell cache.
::

As soon as the cache's gets sold, Leadership opens up their old ass ledger spreadsheet and start calculating the rewards of each member's participation in war. Depending on the war rewards, the payout can be very hefty or else it will be peanuts.

The ledger is used to calculate rewards for war hits, chains hits and assists(whenever necessary).

After all the calculations, ledger containing the payout for each member is shared on discord (see attached screenshot). The balances are updated soon after, directly into everyone's faction vault.

![War Payout Announcement](/images/warPayoutAnnouncement.png)

::tip
If the rewards are good, faction tries to distribute bonus rewards to members for putting their efforts in the war.
::
