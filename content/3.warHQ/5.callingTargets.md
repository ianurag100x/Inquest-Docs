One of the biggest causes of wasted Energy during Ranked Wars is multiple faction members attacking the same opponent simultaneously. This usually happens because nobody communicates.

Sharing targets prevents duplicate attacks and helps distribute available enemies more efficiently.

## Why Call Targets?

Imagine the following situation:

- You are preparing to attack **Enemy A**.
- Another faction member has already selected the same target.
- Neither of you says anything.

One faction member initiates the attack. A few second later another faction member joins the fight. Result: **Wasted Energy**

## How to call target?

Simple communication avoids this entirely. Before attacking, post your intended target in **Discord** or **faction chat**.

Example:

```
Hitting Player123 in 1min
```

or

```
Going for JohnDoe
```

::note
Faction members be wise and don't steal targets. If you still see someone hitting your called out target, you are to tag them and ask them to stop. If they still don't listen, council will handle them.
::
