## Chain Mechanics

Chains are one of the most important mechanics in Torn and are the backbone of every successful Ranked War. A **chain** represents a continuous series of successful attacks performed by the faction within a limited time window. Every successful attack increases the chain count by **one**, regardless of whether it is a War Hit or a Chain Hit.

Maintaining a chain allows the faction to reach valuable **Bonus Hits**, which provide substantial Respect and can often determine the outcome of a close war.

::note
Never let a big chain break! It should be the instict.
::

![Chain](9c659511-31da-4515-bfa9-3fc1030e8b21-3718561.png)

### Starting a Chain

A chain does **not** begin automatically. To initiate one, the faction must land **10 successful attacks** within a **5-minute** period. This initial period is commonly referred to as the **Warm-Up** phase.

Once the 10th hit is landed successfully, the chain officially starts and continues counting indefinitely as long as it is maintained.

### Maintaining the Chain

Once the chain has started, the rules become much simpler. The faction must land: **At least one successful hit every five minutes**

Every successful hit resets the five-minute timer. As long as the timer never expires, the chain continues to grow.

As long as another hit lands before five minutes pass, the chain remains active.

### Chain Cooldown (Breaking the Chain)

If **no successful attack** is made within the five-minute window, the chain immediately ends. This is commonly referred to as the chain **breaking** or going into **Cooldown (CD)**.

No new chain can be initiated until the current chain cooldown(CD) ends.

## Chain Watchers

To reduce the risk of a broken chain, InQuest assigns one or more **Chain Watchers** during active wars.

Their responsibilities include:

- Monitoring the chain timer.
- Alerting members when a hit is urgently needed.
- Preventing accidental chain breaks.

If a Chain Watcher asks for **"any hit"**, make one immediately if you are able.

Even a simple chain hit is better than allowing the chain to expire.

---

## Calling Bonus Hits

As the chain approaches an important milestone, leadership will coordinate attacks to ensure the bonus lands correctly.

You may see announcements such as:

```
Hold at 495
```

or

```
Need four hits
```

These instructions help coordinate exactly who lands the final bonus attack. Avoid attacking unless instructed. **One unnecessary hit can waste an entire bonus.**

---

## Chain Etiquette

During long wars, following a few simple habits helps everyone.

✔ Call your attacks when requested.

✔ Listen for leadership instructions.

✔ Don't rush bonus hits.

✔ Don't ignore chain timers.

✔ Share available targets.

✔ Ask questions if you're unsure.
