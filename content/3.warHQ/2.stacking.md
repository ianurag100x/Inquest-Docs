## Preparing Your Energy (Stacking)

Energy is one of the most valuable resources during Ranked Wars.

Every attack consumes Energy, meaning players with larger Energy stacks can contribute significantly more hits when the war begins.

The maximum stack available is:

| Energy Source               | Maximum |
| --------------------------- | ------: |
| Natural Energy              |    150E |
| Stacked Energy (Max Energy) |   1000E |

The objective is simple:

**Begin the war with as much Energy as possible while finishing your Xanax cooldown before the war starts.**

---

## Method 1 — 1000E Stack (Recommended)

This is the standard method used by most experienced players. It allows you to enter the war with the maximum possible Energy while having no active drug cooldown.

### When to Start

Approximately **32 hours before the scheduled war start.**

### Before Stacking

- Spend your existing Energy in the gym.
- Use your daily Energy refill first if available.
- Ensure your addiction is manageable.

### Stacking Timeline

| Time Before War | Action       | Total Energy |
| --------------- | ------------ | -----------: |
| 32 Hours        | First Xanax  |         250E |
| 24 Hours        | Second Xanax |         500E |
| 16 Hours        | Third Xanax  |         750E |
| 8 Hours         | Fourth Xanax |        1000E |

By war start:

- Drug cooldown has expired.
- Maximum Energy is available.
- No waiting is required.

::tip
War leader or leadership will most likely announcement when it is a good time to stack incase some members forgget to stack.
::

---

## Method 2 — 900E Stack

Sometimes players cannot begin stacking early enough.

The 900E method is the safer alternative.

Start with full natural Energy (150E) approximately **24 hours before war**.

| Time Before War | Action       | Total Energy |
| --------------- | ------------ | -----------: |
| 24 Hours        | First Xanax  |         400E |
| 16 Hours        | Second Xanax |         650E |
| 8 Hours         | Third Xanax  |         900E |

While this produces slightly less Energy than the 1000E method, it is easier to manage and still provides an excellent starting position.

::tip
Leadership will usually remind everyone when stacking should begin. Keep Discord notifications enabled before every scheduled war.
::

---

## Overdosing (OD)

Every Xanax carries a chance of overdosing.

Unfortunately, there is no strategy that completely prevents an OD.

If you overdose during your stacking window:

- You lose valuable preparation time.
- Your Energy stack is ruined.
- Drug cooldown timings become unpredictable.

In short:

> **You're fooked.**
