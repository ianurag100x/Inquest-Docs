Before anything InQuest values real life first, One of the core principles of InQuest is that **real life always takes priority over Torn.**

Unexpected work, family commitments, exams, illness, or emergencies happen. Nobody is expected to ignore real-life responsibilities for a game.

If you cannot participate in a war:

- Inform [**Cheez**](https://www.torn.com/profiles.php?XID=2751561) or [**Carrion**](https://www.torn.com/profiles.php?XID=588903) beforehand.
- A simple message is enough.
- This helps leadership plan the war roster and adjust expectations.

::note
Being unavailable is never an issue. Disappearing without notice makes coordination difficult. Communication is always appreciated.
::

## Ranked War Guide

This guide explains everything an **InQuest** member needs to know before participating in Ranked Wars. Whether it's your first war or your hundredth, following these guidelines ensures better coordination, higher respect gain, and maximum rewards for the entire faction.

### How InQuest Runs Ranked Wars

Every faction develops its own playstyle. The following rules are specific to **InQuest** and should always be followed unless leadership announces otherwise.

| Rule             | Details                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| War Schedule     | Ranked Wars are usually fought every weekend.                                                              |
| War Type         | Most wars are **non-termed**, unless announced otherwise.                                                  |
| Travel           | Do **not** travel during war unless leadership specifically requests it.                                   |
| War Announcement | Final war confirmation and stacking instructions are normally posted **1.5–2 days before war** on Discord. |
| Xanax            | Only use **Faction Armory Xanax** for stacking. Save your own supply.                                      |
| Friendly Actions | You may be hospitalized or even bountied by faction members during coordinated strategies. This is normal. |
| Equipment        | Always borrow a **Warlord weapon** from the armory before attacking whenever available.                    |
| Attack Result    | Unless instructed otherwise, **Leave** or **Hospitalize** your target. Never Mug during Ranked Wars.       |
| Rewards          | Cache sales and payout distributions are announced on Discord after the war concludes.                     |
| Questions        | If you're unsure about anything, ask in Faction Chat or Discord before acting.                             |

A snapshot of what war announcement looks like, it will have your local war start time (not the TCT one) and a basic strategy. Remember not every war is different, all we have to do is to efficient.

:::accordion-item{label="Things to do before the War kicks in" icon="i-lucide-circle-help"}

Preparing properly before a Ranked War is just as important as participating in one. Good preparation means entering the war with maximum Energy, minimal addiction, the correct equipment, and an understanding of the war plan.

### 1. Rehab Before War

Your addiction level directly affects your ability to stack Xanax and recover efficiently.

During an active Ranked War, you may not have an opportunity to visit rehab without sacrificing valuable participation time. Because of this, **always rehab before stacking** if your addiction is becoming significant.

::tip
Enter every Ranked War with the **lowest addiction possible**. This gives you the greatest flexibility during long wars and reduces unnecessary drug cooldown management.

::

### 2. Turn Revives OFF

- Before every Ranked War, **disable revives**.
- Reviving enemy players during an active war can directly help the opposing faction and may interfere with coordinated pushes. Always double-check that revives are disabled before the war begins.

![Hospital Page](/images/hospitalPage.png)

### 3. Spend Your Full Nerve

Always go into war with 0N, chances are you will never get out of hosp for many hours. So better make use of the nerve before the war start so you don't have to worry about having a full nerve bar getting wasted completely.

# Final Notes

Ranked Wars are won through teamwork—not individual performance. Players who communicate, follow leadership instructions, and use their Energy efficiently contribute far more than players who simply attack without coordination.

Remember:

- Prepare before every war.
- Stack correctly.
- Listen for leadership.
- Respect Hold calls.
- Communicate your targets.
- Support your faction.

::tip
Every hit counts. Every member matters.
::
