Not every stage of a Ranked War involves attacking. Sometimes the best strategy is preventing the opposing faction from scoring. This defensive strategy is commonly known as **Turtling** (or Self-Hospitalizing).

Instead of being out, getting hit by opposing faction and bleeding the war score, it is advised to stay in hospital so you do not get hit. Every minute the enemy cannot attack you is a minute they are not earning Respect from you.

There are several methods of turtling referenced below:

## Method 1 — Ipecac Syrup

This is the most commonly used method.

### Benefits

- Easily available through the Faction Armory.
- Provides over one hour of hospital time.
- Quick to use.

### Drawbacks

- Adds approximately **30 minutes of Medical Cooldown.**

::warning
Don't continuously consume Ipecac every few minutes. It will only increase you **medical cooldown (med CD)** and won't help much in hospital time.
::

## Method 2 — Blood Bags

Blood Bags are another reliable method of self-hospitalizing.

Requirements:

- You can only use them if you have done the education: `Biolody: Intravenous Therapy`
- Use an **incompatible blood type**

### Benefits

- Over one hour of hospital time.
- Reliable once prepared.

### Drawbacks

- Adds approximately **30 minutes of Medical Cooldown.**

To discover your compatible blood type, simply fill an empty Blood Bag first.

Afterwards, you'll know which blood types are incompatible and can be used to self-hospitalize.

::note
Use ipecac and blood bag once every hour, do not continuously chug them.
::

## Method 3 — Travelling

Travelling is strictly prohibited during wars, exception only if you are super-active member. Travelling is one of the safest defensive options **if used correctly**. While travelling, enemy players have fewer opportunities to attack you. However, travelling without checking enemy locations can quickly backfire.

Before travelling, ask yourself:

- Is an enemy already in that country?
- Can I defeat them if necessary?
- Will travelling prevent me from participating when leadership calls?

Because travel time varies, this method provides an unpredictable amount of protection.

::warning
Never travel during war simply because you're bored.
Travel should always support the current war strategy.
::

## Refreshing Hospital Time

If your hospital timer is about to expire while leadership still wants players turtling, you can extend your stay by again using:

- Ipecac Syrup
- Blood Bags

Both items can be used while already hospitalized. This allows you to remain protected without exposing yourself between hospital timers.
