We are one of the TOP **HOF**(Hall of Fame) factions, we have established rules to maintain the integrity of faction and keep it organized. All members are strictly required to follow the rules mentioned below.

::note{}
Every member is monitored by the council, do not think your actions are getting unnoticed. From helping faction in wars to exploiting faction permissions, every action is supervised. You get awarded for what you do.
::

## InQ Rulebook

### General Rules

- Be a good sport and be mindful of your action in TORN.
- We do not tolerate bigotry of any kind. If you are found genuinely harassing someone for their heritage, skin color, religious beliefs, or sexuality, you will immediately be removed from the faction and banned with no warning.
- Faction leadership do best to mitigate drama. Just don't start it, because both sides will be heard.
- We're not pro bullying, but not against bullying the bullies.
- No Flying during wars unless approved by council or war leader.
- Mendatory to read discord announcements and follow the same.
- Follow [armory usage](/my-docs/content/en/3.lobby/2.armory.md) rules always.

### Requirements

- Always read the faction newsletter.
- Taking part in wars is a requirement. (Exception made for irl events and beforehand communication)
- **[Discord](https://discord.gg/mkrjyfyW)** is our main communication channel. Every member is required to join Discord.
- Joining **Tornstats** helps the faction to keep track of your progress. [Check how to join TornStats](https://ui.nuxt.com)
- **OC Requirements**: Always participate in a OC. No Travelling during OC initiation.
- No Travel during wars unless instructed or approved by council.

::caution{}
Unexplained or excessive inactivity will be removed from the faction as it will be a detriment to competitive warring.
::
