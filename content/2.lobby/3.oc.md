**[Organized Crimes](https://www.torn.com/factions.php?step=your#/tab=crimes)** are one of the easiest passive way to make money in TORN. If you have high enough skills, you can earn millions just from OC's. Thus, InQuest takes OC participation seriously.

![ocPlanning](/images/ocPlanning.png)

## Conditions to join an OC

::note
Every single member is required to join and be active in an OC.
::

| OC Level         | Criteria to Join |
| ---------------- | ---------------- |
| `Lvl 1`          | Open to Anyone.  |
| All other levels | CPR above 70     |

- Any item required for OC is provided by the faction. You can loan it from utility armory yourself or someone with armory loaning permission will provide you.
- OC payout rate is 90%. (10% is kept by the faction to cover expenses)
- Once your OC successfully completes, your balance will get updated by members with officer or above role.
- The balance gets stored in faction vault always.
- You can choose to ask for your money via discord, or let the balance build up.

### No Flying When OC about to initiate

Flying stops the OC from initiation. If someone participating in the specific OC choose to fly, other members will likely suffer. You don't want to be the one getting name and shame.

It is advised to not fly if your OC inititation time is around 4-5hrs. No one is allowed to fly during OC initiated

::tip{to="https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/566888-torn-oc-flight-guard"}
OC Flight Guard script helps you prevent from accidental travel. If you are a frequent flyer, install the script. Works for desktop, and tornPDA.
::

![OC Flight Guard](/images/ocFlightGuard.png)

If you are found flying during OC,

- <span style="color: #8b5cf6;">**First Warning :**</span> A warning message from OC admin.
- <span style="color: #fd7404;">**Second Warning :**</span> You will not get paid for the OC.
- <span style="color: #e80d0d;">**Third Warning :**</span> Your case goes to leadership and high chances to get kicked.
