**[Faction armory](https://www.torn.com/factions.php?step=your#/tab=armoury)** is full with RW weapons, consumables, medical supplies, utlities and miscellaneous. It is members duty to make sure the armory and vault usage rules are followed.

Secondly, Faction vault is open to everyone you can deposit money anytime to keep it safe. Keep in mind faction vault isn't your personal vault.

![factionArmory](/images/armory.png)

## Armory Usage

Each of the armory categorizes have specific usage criteria, follow them properly. The armory is always open to every member except for few expensive stuff like xanax.

| Category            | Usage Instructions                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Weapons and Armor` | Open to Everyone. Feel free to loan yourself anything unused, and if someone is using what you want, ask them, don't just take it. |
| `Temporary`         | Open to Everyone. Be mindful how much you loan yourself                                                                            |
| `Consumable`        | Open to Everyone. Except cans.                                                                                                     |
| `Drugs`             | Open to Everyone. Except xanax (available only during wars).                                                                       |
| `Booster`           | Limited. Only use Lawyer Business Card                                                                                             |
| `Utility`           | Open to Everyone.                                                                                                                  |
| `Points`            | Available Only during wars                                                                                                         |

If you notice faction running low on something ping council or any leadership and we'll replenish asap.

::warning
**Do not retrieve** things from any other faction members. Unless approved by them or faction council. Exception for weapons only.
::

::caution{}
First time armory usage violation will lead to a warning. Second time, member will be kicked from the faction unless taken accountability.
::

## Vault Usage

You can always deposit money in faction vault anytime. Vault is open 24/7. However, the money you deposit can only be withdrawn by a member with [treasurer]() perk.

To withdraw your money from faction vault, tag a treasuer on discord. And they will send you your balance from faction vault. Keep in mind delays can be there.

![Treasurer Ping](/images/treasurerPing.png)

::note
We advise you to keep your money to yourself, and not in the faction vault.
::
